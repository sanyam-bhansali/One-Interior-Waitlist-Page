Why we started this

Everyone knows someone this went wrong for.

The advance that was paid and then nobody picked up. The three-month job still unfinished in month nine. The final bill that looked nothing like the first quote.

It shouldn't be this frightening to do up your own home.

So we meet the studios before you ever do. We sit in homes they finished, call the families who paid them, and look at the parts of the work that don't photograph well. What we learn, you get to see — including the things a studio would rather we left out.

And on your first call, an architect sits on your side of the table. Not to sell you anything. Just so that the first person explaining your own home to you isn't someone hoping to be paid for it.

You'll still choose. We just want it to be a safe choice.
