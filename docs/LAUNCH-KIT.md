## 1. Goal and maths

**Goal:** 2,000 people on the Pune list. That's the gate: Pune opens at 2,000, and not before.

### Channel targets

| Channel | Target signups | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Meta ads (₹40,000) | 500–800 | **ASSUMPTION:** ₹50–80 per signup. Check against real cost after week 1 and re-plan. |
| Society drives (posters, standees, RWA groups) | 600–800 | ~25–40 societies × 20–25 signups each |
| Referrals (personal links) | 300–500 | Share rate × signups per sharer (see KPIs) |
| Partnerships (loan agents, brokers, builders, movers) | 100–200 | Tracked links per partner |
| **Total** | **~1,500–2,300** | Aim high. Ads alone won't get us there. |

If week-1 cost per signup comes in above ₹80, move effort (not money) into societies and referrals. Don't try to buy the gap.

### Weekly milestones (8-week plan)

| Week | Cumulative target | Main work |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 150 | ₹10,000 ad test; first 5 societies; founding-list friends and family |
| 2 | 350 | Scale winning ads; 10 societies live; referral nudges begin |
| 3 | 600 | Page now shows "N of 2,000", so use it in every message |

| 8 | 2,000 | Launch (Section 8) |

### KPIs (track weekly)

| KPI | How to measure |
|---|---|
| Signup rate by source | Signups ÷ landing visits, split by `?s=`, `?r=`, `utm_source` |
| Share rate | % of signups whose referral link brings ≥1 visit |
| Signups per sharer | Referred signups ÷ number of people who shared |
| % answering the 3 taps | Signups with possession + BHK + society/style filled ÷ all signups |
| % with keys | "Have keys" answers ÷ people who answered possession |
| Cost per signup (ads) | Meta spend ÷ ad-sourced signups |
| Cost per keys-in-hand signup | Meta spend ÷ ad-sourced signups answering "have keys" |

Cost per keys-in-hand signup matters more than cost per signup. Someone who already has the keys is close to booking.

## 2. The ₹40,000 Meta plan

### Structure

**Week 1: test (₹10,000)**
- 4 creatives (A–D below) × 2 audiences = 8 ad sets, about ₹1,250 each over 7 days.
- **Audience 1, geo:** 2–3 km radius pins around target societies in Kharadi, Wagholi, Hinjewadi, Baner, Wakad, Undri, Keshav Nagar, Hadapsar/Magarpatta, Balewadi, Pimpri-Chinchwad. Age 25–45.
- **Audience 2, geo + interests:** same pins, age 25–45, interests such as new home, home loans, interior design, home décor, real estate.
- Optimise for the lead/signup conversion event. Placements: Reels, Stories, Feed.

**Weeks 2–5: scale (₹30,000)**
- Put the money into the 2–3 best creative × audience pairs.
- Refresh a creative when frequency passes about 3 or cost per signup rises 30% week on week.
- Keep about ₹5,000 back for weeks 6–7 to support society-unlock pushes.

### Keep / kill rules (check on day 4 and day 7)

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Cost per signup ≤ ₹60 and ≥ 10 signups | **Keep**, and scale 20–30% every 2–3 days |
| ₹60–100 | Keep one more week and swap the hook or headline |
| > ₹100 after ₹1,000 spent, or 0 signups after ₹600 | **Kill** |
| Good cost per signup but < 20% "have keys" | Keep only if cost per keys-in-hand signup is still the best |

## 9. Compliance checklist

Tick every line before any ad, poster or message goes out.

**Consent and data (DPDP Act 2023)**
- [ ] The signup form has an **unticked** consent box with plain wording, e.g. "I agree to One Interiors contacting me on WhatsApp/email about the Pune launch. I can opt out any time."
- [ ] The form links to a privacy notice: what we collect (name, WhatsApp number or email, optional answers), why, how long we keep it, and how to ask for deletion.
