# Studio Listing Agreement

**One Interiors — studio onboarding e-agreement**
Draft v0.1 · for legal review before use

> ⚠️ **This is a drafting starting point, not legal advice, and I am not a lawyer.**
> It is a binding commercial contract governed by Indian law and it must be reviewed by an
> advocate qualified in India before a single studio signs it. The clauses most likely to
> need professional attention are marked **[REVIEW]**. Section 16 lists what a lawyer will
> want to decide.

---

## How this is meant to work in the product

Presented as **step 5 of studio onboarding**, after profile, registration, portfolio and
rate card — so the studio has already entered everything the agreement refers to.

Acceptance is recorded as the `CODE_OF_CONDUCT` check in the fifteen. Store, per
acceptance: the full agreement text as shown, its version, a SHA-256 of that text, the
accepted timestamp, the accepting person's name and role, the account email, and the IP
address. **Never store just a boolean** — you must be able to reproduce the exact document
a studio agreed to, years later.

Scrolling to the end should be required before the accept control enables. Tick boxes must
be unticked by default. Both are evidentiary, not decorative.

# STUDIO LISTING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between:

**(1) [ONE INTERIORS LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a [company type] incorporated under the
Companies Act, 2013, CIN [CIN], having its registered office at [ADDRESS] ("**One
Interiors**", "**we**", "**us**"); and

**(2) [STUDIO LEGAL NAME]**, [entity type], [CIN / LLPIN / proprietorship], GSTIN [GSTIN],
having its principal place of business at [ADDRESS], acting through [NAME], [DESIGNATION]
("**the Studio**", "**you**").

Accepted electronically on [DATE] and valid under section 10A of the Information Technology
Act, 2000.

---

## 1 · What this Agreement covers

1.1 One Interiors operates a marketplace that matches homeowners with interior design
studios, verifies claims about those studios, generates indicative quotations from each
studio's own filed rate card, and introduces customers to studios.

1.2 This Agreement governs your listing on that marketplace. It does not govern the design
or build contract between you and a customer, which is yours alone (see clause 7).

1.3 **We are an introducer, not a party to your customer contracts.** We do not design, we
do not build, we do not supervise, and we do not warrant your work.

---

## 2 · Listing, and what a listing is not

2.1 Listing is at our discretion. Applying does not entitle you to be listed.

2.2 **Nobody can pay to rank higher.** Match order is produced by our scoring engine from
the customer's answers — locality, scope, budget band, style leaning, household — and from
how many of the fifteen verification checks you have cleared. No commercial arrangement
moves a studio up a customer's results.

2.3 A subscription, where offered, buys **volume** — how many customer briefs you are put in
front of. It never buys position. You may see your own delivered volume in your dashboard.

2.4 We may pause your listing at any time under clause 11. A paused studio is removed from
matching; pausing never promotes another studio.

---

## 3 · Verification

3.1 You authorise us to carry out, and to repeat at any time, the fifteen verification
checks:

**Identity** — PAN and legal name match · Aadhaar KYC of the principal · registered address
visited · working number and email · this Agreement signed.

**Trading history** — GSTIN active · twelve months of GST filings · company filings current
(MCA) · Udyam registration · three past clients called · two finished sites inspected ·
litigation and consumer forum search.

**What the customer is owed** — rate card filed and locked · workmanship warranty on paper ·
labour insurance and site safety.

3.2 You authorise us to obtain this information from Protean, IDFY, the GST Portal, MCA,
the Udyam Portal, eCourts, the NCDRC, and from your named past clients, and to verify it by
visiting your premises and completed sites. **[REVIEW: a lawyer should confirm the consent
language required for each source, particularly Aadhaar, which is regulated under the
Aadhaar Act, 2016 and may require offline KYC or a licensed KUA.]**

3.3 **We publish the results, including the ones that do not flatter you.** A customer sees
how many checks you have cleared, which ones are outstanding, the source of each and the
date it was checked. We also publish delivered project counts and average schedule overrun.

3.4 **You cannot pay to alter, suppress or accelerate any check or published metric.** Any
attempt to do so is a material breach under clause 11.2.

3.5 You must tell us within **seven days** if anything verified stops being true — a lapsed
GST registration, a changed principal, a new consumer complaint, a lapsed insurance policy.

---

## 4 · Your obligations

You agree to:

4.1 Give accurate, complete and current information, and correct it when it changes.

4.2 **Own or be licensed for every portfolio image you upload**, and have the consent of the
homeowner shown. You are solely responsible for third-party claims arising from what you
upload.

4.3 **Not present another studio's work as your own.** This is a material breach.

4.4 Respond to an introduced customer within **[X business hours — commercial decision]**.

4.5 Hold and maintain the registrations you have claimed, including GST where applicable.

4.6 Carry workmanship warranty terms in writing and labour insurance covering your sites,
and produce evidence on request.

4.7 Comply with applicable law, including the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the Real Estate
(Regulation and Development) Act where it applies, GST law, and labour and site-safety law.

4.8 **Not solicit an introduced customer off-platform to avoid the fee in clause 6.** See
clause 6.5.

4.9 Not misrepresent your relationship with us. You are a listed studio, not an agent,
partner, employee or franchisee of One Interiors.

---

## 5 · Rate card

5.1 You file a rate card. We generate indicative quotations from it. **We do not set, alter
or negotiate your prices.**

5.2 Once filed, your rate card is **locked** for the period in clause 5.3, so that a
quotation shown to a customer cannot be different from the one you filed.

5.3 You may revise it **[frequency — commercial decision, suggest once per quarter with
14 days' notice]**. Revisions do not affect quotations already issued.

5.4 A quotation generated from your rate card is **indicative**, computed from the
customer's stated carpet area and scope. It is not an offer by you and does not bind you
until you issue your own quotation. Site conditions may change the figure, and the customer
is told this.

5.5 **You must honour your filed rate card** for a customer introduced through us, for the
scope quoted, save for changes properly documented under your own contract with them.

---

## 6 · Fees

6.1 **Commission.** You pay **4.00% (four hundred basis points)** of the net contract value
of work you undertake for a customer introduced through One Interiors. This is the pilot
rate.

6.2 **GST at 18%** applies to our fee and is charged in addition.

6.3 **Subscription**, where you take one, is billed as set out in your subscription schedule
and buys volume only (clause 2.3).

6.4 **Payment terms.** Commission falls due on **[trigger — commercial decision: customer
signature, first milestone payment, or project completion]** and is payable within
**[X days]**. **[REVIEW: the trigger materially changes your cash flow and your risk if a
project is abandoned mid-way.]**

6.5 **Circumvention.** If, within **twelve months** of an introduction, you contract with
that customer outside the platform for substantially the work introduced, the commission in
6.1 remains payable. **[REVIEW: enforceability and the window are for a lawyer; twelve
months is common but must be reasonable to be enforceable.]**

6.6 We may change the pilot rate on **[X days']** written notice. A change does not apply to
customers already introduced.

---

## 7 · You contract with the customer, not us

7.1 Any design or build contract is **between you and the customer directly.** One
Interiors is not a party to it.

7.2 **You are solely responsible** for the work, its quality, its timeline, its cost, site
safety, your workers and subcontractors, materials, warranty, and any dispute with the
customer.

7.3 We may facilitate an architect consultation for the customer before they commit. That
architect advises the customer, not you, and their review is not approval, supervision, or
acceptance of responsibility for your work by us.

7.4 **Escrow.** Where the customer uses our escrow facility, a customer-side escrow fee of
**1.75%** applies and payments release on the milestones the customer and you agree. Escrow
does not make us a party to your contract.

---

## 8 · Data

8.1 **Customer data.** A brief shared with you is confidential and may be used only to quote
for and deliver that project. You may not sell it, share it, or use it for marketing
without the customer's separate consent. You must delete it on request unless law requires
retention.

8.2 You are an independent data fiduciary in respect of customer data you receive, under the
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and are responsible for your own compliance.
**[REVIEW: whether the relationship is fiduciary-to-fiduciary or fiduciary-to-processor
changes obligations on both sides and should be decided deliberately.]**

8.3 **Your data.** You licence us to display your business name, locality, team size, years
trading, portfolio images, rate-card-derived indicative pricing, verification results,
delivered project counts and schedule performance, for the purpose of operating the
marketplace and for marketing it.

8.4 **Verification records are not deleted on delisting.** They are retained as part of the
integrity of the marketplace, and may be reported to a customer who was introduced to you
while you were listed. **[REVIEW: retention must be squared with DPDPA erasure rights;
there are legitimate grounds but they need to be stated properly.]**

8.5 A breach notification reaches us within **seventy-two hours** of you becoming aware.

---

## 9 · Confidentiality

9.1 Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential and uses it only for
this Agreement. This survives termination by **[X] years**.

9.2 Our scoring logic, verification methodology and unpublished roster data are our
confidential information.

---

## 10 · Intellectual property

10.1 You keep ownership of your designs, drawings and portfolio.

10.2 You grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use, reproduce and
display your portfolio images and business information for operating and marketing the
marketplace, for as long as you are listed and for **[X months]** after, for archival and
case-study purposes.

10.3 We keep ownership of the platform, the scoring engine, the quotation engine, the
verification framework and all associated IP.

10.4 Neither party may use the other's trade marks except to describe the relationship
truthfully.

---

## 11 · Suspension and delisting

11.1 **Pausing.** We may pause your listing immediately where a verification check fails or
lapses, where a customer complaint is under investigation, where a registration expires, or
where we reasonably believe a term is being breached. A pause removes you from matching. We
tell you why.

11.2 **Material breach**, permitting immediate delisting:

- misrepresenting your identity, registrations or trading history
- passing off another studio's work
- attempting to pay for, or induce, a change to verification results or match position
- circumventing clause 6.5
- a finding against you by a consumer forum for work introduced through us
- conduct causing material harm to a customer
- failing to honour your filed rate card

11.3 **Appeal.** You may appeal a pause or delisting within **fourteen days** in writing. We
will review and respond within **fourteen days** with reasons. **[REVIEW: consider whether
the appeal should go to someone other than the original decision-maker.]**

11.4 **On delisting** your listing is removed and no new introductions are made. Obligations
in respect of customers already introduced survive, including clause 6.

11.5 **You may leave** on **thirty days'** written notice. Commission on customers already
introduced remains payable.

## 12 · Liability

12.1 **[REVIEW — this clause needs a lawyer's attention more than any other in the
document.]**

12.2 Nothing here excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence,
fraud, or anything that cannot lawfully be excluded.

12.3 Subject to 12.2, our aggregate liability to you in any twelve-month period is limited
to the total fees you paid us in that period.

12.4 Subject to 12.2, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of
profit, or loss of business opportunity.

12.5 **You indemnify us** against claims from a customer or third party arising from your
work, your breach of this Agreement, your portfolio uploads, or your handling of customer
data. **[REVIEW: an uncapped indemnity against a capped liability is a common point of
negotiation and studios' advisors will push back.]**

## 13 · Relationship

13.1 You are an independent contractor. Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture,
agency or employment relationship.

13.2 You have no authority to bind us or to represent yourself as authorised to.

---

## 14 · Changes to this Agreement

14.1 We may amend it on **thirty days'** written notice to your registered email.

14.2 If you do not accept an amendment you may terminate under clause 11.5 before it takes
effect. Continuing to accept introductions after it takes effect is acceptance.

14.3 **[REVIEW: unilateral amendment rights in a standard-form contract attract scrutiny
under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and consumer/unfair-terms principles. Consider carving
out fee changes for separate consent.]**

---

## 15 · Governing law and disputes

15.1 Governed by the laws of India.

15.2 Courts at **[Pune, Maharashtra]** have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to 15.3.

15.3 Disputes are first referred to good-faith discussion between senior representatives for
**thirty days**, then to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at **[Pune]**, conducted in English. **[REVIEW: arbitration
may be disproportionate for small-value disputes with small studios — consider a value
threshold below which courts apply.]**

15.4 Notices go to the registered email of each party and are deemed received on the next
business day.

# What a lawyer needs to decide

Take this list to them with the draft.

| # | Decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aadhaar KYC consent** (3.2) | Regulated under the Aadhaar Act 2016. You may need offline KYC or licensed KUA status. This is the single biggest compliance risk in the document. |
| 2 | **Commission trigger** (6.4) | Signature vs first milestone vs completion completely changes your cash flow and your exposure to abandoned projects. |
| 3 | **Circumvention window** (6.5) | Must be reasonable to be enforceable. Twelve months is common; too long and a court may strike it. |
| 4 | **Liability cap vs indemnity** (12.3 / 12.5) | Capped liability alongside an uncapped indemnity is the clause studios' advisors will fight. Decide your position before you meet resistance. |
| 5 | **DPDPA roles** (8.2) | Fiduciary-to-fiduciary or fiduciary-to-processor changes both sides' obligations materially. |
| 6 | **Retaining verification records after delisting** (8.4) | Squaring retention with erasure rights needs a properly stated lawful basis. |
| 7 | **Unilateral amendment** (14) | Standard-form amendment rights attract unfair-terms scrutiny. Consider separate consent for fee changes. |
| 8 | **Arbitration threshold** (15.3) | Arbitration costs can exceed the dispute value for a small studio. A value threshold is usually fairer and more enforceable. |

**Also settle commercially before signing anyone:**
response-time SLA (4.4) · rate card revision frequency (5.3) · payment days (6.4) ·
notice period for fee changes (6.6) · portfolio licence tail (10.2) · confidentiality
survival (9.1).

## Why the agreement reads the way it does

Most marketplace agreements are one-directional: every obligation runs from the supplier to
the platform. This one deliberately writes down what **we** are bound to as well — that
nobody can buy position (2.2), that a subscription buys volume only (2.3), that we publish
results as found and cannot be paid to change them (3.4), that pausing never promotes
someone else (2.4), and that there is a real appeal (11.3).

That is not generosity. A studio is being asked to submit to fifteen checks and have its
schedule overruns published. The only thing that makes that a reasonable ask is a contract
that binds the platform to the same standard of honesty it is demanding. It is also the
clause set a good studio will read first.
